Behind The Counter

One Core Recipe. Endless Happiness.

At The Muffin Stop, we believe in doing one thing exceptionally well: creating the world’s most delicious banana muffins.

Every morning begins with caramelizing naturally sweet, over-ripe bananas into a silky batter. From crunchy toasted walnuts to dark chocolate drizzle and cold banana ice cream, we experiment with small artisanal twists that elevate the humble muffin into a cozy delicacy.
